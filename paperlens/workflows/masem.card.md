## Introduction

The MASEMiner direct-information preset, run as a process: every new paper is extracted by the model on the worker, the declared contract is re-checked on the stored values, and every new entry is reviewed before a release.

## Steps

1. **Direct information** (live). One model call per paper with the `masem-direct` preset.
2. **Contract check** (deterministic). The declared contract re-checked on the stored values.
3. **Release**, gated on the review of every new entry.

## Review

Every entry of a new paper is reviewed before the release.
