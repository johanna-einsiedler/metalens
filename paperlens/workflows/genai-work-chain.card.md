## Introduction

The GenAI-at-work review extracts, from every included paper, what the paper reports about generative AI and work: the study's design, the units of its estimates — tasks, arms, outcomes, subgroups, horizons — the estimates themselves, read from the paper's exhibits, and the claims of its abstract mapped to the estimates that support them. In this workflow the extraction runs inside Metalens: the same prompts the review project ran locally, as pass steps over the uploaded PDF, the same deterministic code assembling and checking the record, and the same three validation tasks over it.

A pass is registered code that runs once per paper, may call the model, reads the page-marked text and the outputs of the earlier passes of the same paper, and returns one JSON object. Its prompt files pin it: a change to a prompt or a schema is a new pin and a new version of the workflow. Four passes are read more than once, and a matcher folds the readings into the one the later passes read.

## Steps

1. **Exhibits as printed** (pass b, two readings). Every table and figure transcribed — captions, panels, headers, rows, the figures' numbers — and the numbers stated in the text. The first reading is the primary; the second is kept beside it.
2. **Estimands** (pass a, two readings). The tasks, the arms of each task with the reference arm first, the outcomes, and one estimand per task × arm × reference × outcome × subgroup × horizon, each with the sentence that reports it. The second reading is aligned to the first by content — tasks by name, arms by label and use, outcomes by measure, estimands by structure and wording — and what the alignment leaves unmatched on both sides goes to the model once.
3. **Estimates** (pass c, two readings). Every estimate of every estimand read from the exhibits: the numbers as printed, the cell they came from, the role (main, supporting, robustness) and the sentence that states it. A long list is asked in parts and completed. The second reading is compared estimand by estimand; a row the readings disagree on is marked on the record.
4. **Study, task and arm variables** (pass d, three readings). The study's design variables, each task's category and complexity descriptions, each arm's sample and AI, every cell with its quote. Each cell follows the majority of the three readings: a dissent is noted as an uncertainty marker, no majority is flagged for a coder.
5. **The abstract's claims** (two calls). The abstract's sentences classified and matched to the introduction's, the result claims mapped to the record's estimates with how far the record backs them, every stated number looked up among the printed ones.
6. **Headline plan** (four to five calls). Per unit — an outcome category of one task — the passages describing the task, the level the unit is analysed at and what the appendix may hold, the abstract's atomic claims (the discussion's for the units the abstract does not cover), the number behind each claim traced to a printed cell or a formula over printed cells and checked in code, and the rule's decision that the headline rule reads.
7. **Extraction record** (code, free). The record assembled from the passes: the derived cells (finding types from measure kinds, contrasts from the reference arm, the main role of an only estimate), the canonical ids, the study from pass d, each task with its arms and outcomes, one reference row and one contrast row per estimate, every quote verified against the page text and snapped to the paper's wording when edited. It becomes the extraction document under the same contract as the supplied records of version 4 — with the units' own words, the composite outcomes, the abstract's claims matched to their units, every exhibit with its cited cells and its transcription — and the readings' disagreements painted on the record card.
8. **Record checks** and **Chain checks** (deterministic, free). The record check runs over the document as stored. The chain check runs the project's whole issue list over the four passes: the record's checks on the record assembled from them, the sources resolved into the transcribed exhibits, the exhibit coverage and the grid gaps, the duplicates, the statistics against each other, the table and the stating sentence, the readings' disagreements. Findings are notes per paper.
9. **Routing** (code, free). A paper is routed when its record contradicts an inclusion criterion — every extracted size below 200, no no-AI reference arm, no performance outcome, no identification — or when a headline estimate scores at least the threshold on the triage: 2 per failed check, 1 per warning, 1 per field the readings disagree on, 2 when a reading lacks it. The flags table lists what a coder resolves, each flag with its page; the decision, its reasons and the paper's highest triage score are written on the document.
10. **Judgment** (template `genai-work-judgment`, two coders per paper). The study's screening criteria and design from the PDF alone, the units as extracted with the coder's categories, the abstract's sentences against their units — two coders per paper, blind to each other and to the model's categories.
11. **Extraction check** (template `genai-work-check`, one coder per paper). One table or figure drawn at random among those an estimate cites; its cited cells confirmed against the print, the missed ones added, a full-review flag.
12. **Routed papers** (template `genai-work-routed`, one coder per paper). Every flag of a routed paper resolved with a verdict and, where it applies, a corrected value.
13. **Contract check** (deterministic, free). The declared fields, types and options re-checked on the values as stored, after the coders' corrections.
14. **Release**. Gated on the record and the three tasks.

## Review

The three tasks are those of version 4, unchanged: whole papers (`unit: paper`; the check draws one row of the paper's exhibits), the judgment assigned to two of the project's people, the other two tasks to one, coders assigned in turn, no coder seeing another's coding. A coder's answers are their coding; the record as assembled stays as it was.

## Versions

The workflow pins each pass by the hash of its prompt files and schema, the extraction preset by its data contract, and names the three templates. A change to a prompt, a schema, the taxonomy snapshot, a pass's inputs, the number of readings, a step, a template's questions or a review declaration is a new version; a change of wording is not. Version 1 runs the chain of the review project as of 20 November 2026 (split prompt 2b9af957a9, design prompt afbb8492d2); the supplied-record workflow `genai-work` version 4 keeps its id for the records the project extracted locally. Version 2 (20 November 2026) follows the project's taxonomy amendment of that day: `research_design` gains `quasi_experiment`, the arms' `sample_population_type` gains `ai_alone`, the free terms an amendment folded into a code are read as that code, a unit with an AI-alone arm on either side gets no headline, and the extraction contract is v21 (design prompt c10c2460c9).
